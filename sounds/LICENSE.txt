FRONT ZERO - recorded weapon sounds

Every file in this folder (shots/ and handling/) is an edit of recordings from:

  The Free Firearm Sound Library
  Recorded and released by Ben Jaszczak and the contributors to the 2014 Kickstarter project "The Firearm Sound Library".
  Licence: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Sources:
  https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
  https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary (mirror; its LICENSE file is the CC0 1.0 legal code)
  https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/bjjaszcz/the-firearm-sound-library

CC0 does not require attribution; it is given here as a courtesy and for traceability.
Edits made for the game: takes cut out of the recordings, 25 Hz high-pass, fades, loudness normalisation with at most 3 dB of
look-ahead peak limiting on a few takes, 48 kHz, MP3 (LAME VBR). The edited files are released under the same CC0 1.0 terms.
Files marked "layered edit" (launchers, crossbow, belt, hands, cloth, grenade, knife air) are made only by cutting, layering, pitching,
filtering and fading these same recordings: no synthesized audio is mixed in.
The recordings are of real firearms; the weapons in the game are fictional and are not the recorded models.
